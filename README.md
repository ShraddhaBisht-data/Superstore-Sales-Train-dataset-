# Superstore-Sales EDA

🎯 Why This Project?

Businesses generate huge amounts of sales data every day, but raw data alone cannot support decision-making. This project uses Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) to uncover hidden patterns, understand customer behavior, identify top-performing products, and discover growth opportunities.

🔍 Research Questions

Which products generate the highest sales?

Which customer segment contributes the most?

Which region performs best?

Are sales and profit related?

How can the business improve performance?
 
💡 Hypotheses

Higher sales lead to higher profit.
Some categories perform significantly better than others.
Customer segments contribute differently to sales.
Regional sales performance varies.
Shipping mode influences customer purchasing behavior.

🛠 What Was Done?

✔ Data Cleaning & Preprocessing
✔ Missing Value Treatment
✔ Duplicate Removal
✔ Univariate Analysis
✔ Bivariate Analysis
✔ Correlation Analysis
✔ Business Insight Generation

📈 Key Insights

Certain categories dominate overall sales.
Customer purchasing behavior differs by segment.
Sales performance varies across regions.
Sales and profit show a positive relationship.
Seasonal trends impact business performance.

 Conclusion

This EDA transformed raw sales data into meaningful business insights. The analysis helps identify growth opportunities, improve decision-making, optimize operations, and support data-driven business strategies.

⭐ Final Takeaway

"Data tells the story of a business. Through EDA, we converted sales records into actionable insights that can drive smarter decisions and business growth." 🚀
